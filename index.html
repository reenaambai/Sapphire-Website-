Our Approach

Where strategy becomes reality

Diagnose· Prioritize· Build· Roll Out· Reinforce

Anyone, or any AI tool, can help you build something. AI makes it easier to create and move faster than ever. That makes knowing what to do even more important. But building it is only half the job. Your team has to actually use it, and the change has to stick. That's where I come in.

01
Diagnose

Understand what's actually getting in the way.

Look at the business, the GTM motion, the team, and the data to figure out where performance is breaking down.

02
Prioritize

Focus on what will actually move the business.

There are always more things you could fix. I identify the few that matter most and leave the rest alone.

03
Build

Create what the team actually needs.

Messaging, processes, onboarding, coaching systems, enablement infrastructure, or whatever the diagnosis calls for.

04
Roll Out

Get it into the field.

I work with the team to put the change into practice, test it, adjust it, and make sure people know how to use it.

05
Reinforce

Make it stick.

Build the coaching, measurement, and operating habits that keep the change alive after Sapphire leaves.

About

I started Sapphire because I kept seeing good GTM strategies die in execution.

The strategy would make sense. The team would agree with it. Someone would build the playbook.

And then six months later, reps were still doing what they'd always done.

I've spent my career working with SaaS revenue teams across sales, enablement, and GTM to close that gap. At Salesloft, I was the company's first enablement hire and built the function from the ground up. Since then, I've worked with revenue teams at different stages of growth, helping them turn strategy into something their teams can actually execute.

Today, AI makes it faster and cheaper to build almost anything. I think that makes judgment and experience even more valuable. My job is to help you figure out what matters, get your team behind it, and make sure the change actually sticks.

That's why I keep Sapphire small. I work directly with the teams I support, stay close to the actual work, and take on a limited number of clients at a time.

I don't want to hand you a strategy and walk away. I want to help you turn it into something your team actually runs.
